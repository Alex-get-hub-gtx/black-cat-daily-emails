How long to wait before you walk

Set the date privately.

Most women wait without a deadline, which means they wait indefinitely.

She tells herself she'll give it a bit longer. And a bit longer keeps renewing, because there's never a day on which it expires, and so two years pass in a series of reasonable extensions.

Here's the fix, and it's one of the most useful things I can give you.

Set the date privately, and never tell him.

Not an ultimatum. An ultimatum is a threat, and a threat is a request wearing a costume, and he will hear the request. Ultimatums fail because they tell him there's still time to negotiate.

A private deadline is different. It's a decision you've already made about your own life. He doesn't get a vote because it isn't about him.

Here's how to set it properly.

Pick the behaviour, not the feeling. Not "if things don't improve." Something you could point at. A plan made without you prompting it. Meeting someone who matters to him. A clear answer, unasked for. Something specific that either happens or doesn't.

Pick the date. Real, and on your calendar. Three months is usually right. Long enough to be fair, short enough that you don't lose a year.

Write it down somewhere only you see.

And then, this is the discipline, carry on normally. Don't audit him weekly. Don't drop hints. Don't test him. Just live, and let the date arrive.

Here's why it works so well.

It stops the daily negotiation inside your own head. You're not asking yourself every Sunday whether to stay. That question is answered until the date, which frees up an extraordinary amount of your attention.

And it changes how you are with him, immediately and invisibly. A woman with a private deadline is calmer, less needy, less interested in extracting reassurance, because she's no longer trying to get information out of him. She's just watching. Men respond to that enormously, and sometimes the thing you were waiting for arrives inside the window purely because you stopped chasing it.

And if it doesn't arrive, you leave on a day you chose, rather than on the day you finally broke.

What actually works.

One behaviour, one date, written down, told to nobody.

Three months unless there's a reason for different.

No hints, no tests, no audits in between.

And when the date comes, honour it. This is the whole thing. A deadline you move is just a wish with a calendar entry, and your own trust in yourself is built or lost right here.

A deadline he knows about is a negotiation. A deadline only you know about is a decision.

Your one move today.

If you're waiting on something, set it now. One behaviour, one date, in your notes.

Then close your phone and go and have a normal day.
