The four types of women

Work out which one you are.

Most women have never been given a name for what they're doing.

So they can't change it, because you cannot change a pattern you can't see. Here are the four, and you'll recognise yourself in one of them within about ten seconds.

The golden retriever.

Warm, loyal, endlessly available. She loves hard and early. She organises everything, remembers everything, forgives quickly. She's the one who says "whatever you want" about dinner and means it.

What she gets: taken for granted, every time, by men who genuinely like her. Her problem is not her heart. It's that she's free, and nothing free gets protected.

The black widow.

Cold, strategic, punishing. She's been hurt and she's built a system to make sure it never happens again. She tests constantly. She withholds as a weapon. She wins arguments and loses relationships.

What she gets: short, intense relationships with men who find her thrilling and then leave because nothing is ever safe. She looks like the opposite of the golden retriever. She's actually the same wound with different armour.

The bambi.

Sweet, trusting, a bit naive. She sees the best in everyone, gives the benefit of the doubt by default, and believes words over behaviour. She's the one who says "but he said he was sorry."

What she gets: found. Specifically, found by the men who are looking for exactly this. Her kindness is real and it has no filter attached, and the filter is the thing that keeps her safe.

The black cat.

Warm but not available. Affectionate and not anxious. She has a full life, she has standards with actual consequences, she can be alone on a Tuesday, and she chooses rather than hopes.

She's not cold. That's the misunderstanding everybody makes. She's warm. She just isn't managing anyone's comfort, including her own discomfort about his silence.

What she gets: pursued, and treated well, and able to leave. Which is why she usually doesn't have to.

Here's what I want you to notice.

Most women are not one type. They're a golden retriever who becomes a black widow after a bad one, and then swings back.

That swing is the actual problem. Both ends are reactions. The black cat is the only one of the four that isn't a response to being hurt.

And here's the part nobody says out loud: you cannot get to black cat from black widow by being colder. You get there by building the life. The behaviour is a symptom of the structure, never the other way round.

What actually works.

Name yours honestly. Not the one you want to be. The one you were last Tuesday at 11pm.

Notice your swing. Which type do you become when you've been hurt? That's your pattern, and it's more useful to know than your default.

And stop trying to perform the fourth one. Build the five pillars and the behaviour arrives on its own. Everything else is acting.

The black cat is not a personality you adopt. She's what's left when you stop needing a man's reply to be alright.

Your one move today.

Name your type. One word, to yourself.

Then name the one you swing to. That pair is your whole dating history.

Which one are you? Reply with just the name, I'd love to see the breakdown.

See you tomorrow!
