The five kinds of men

And what each one costs you.

Most women evaluate men individually, from scratch, every time.

Which means she makes the same discovery at month eight, over and over, with a different face.

Here are the five patterns. Learn them once and you'll recognise them in a fortnight instead of a year.

The comfortable one.

Lovely, warm, genuinely fond of you, and will never commit because he already has everything. No malice at all. He's just at a ceiling and it's a nice ceiling.

The cost: two to four years, usually. He's the most expensive of the five because nothing is ever wrong enough to leave.

The collector.

Intense at the start. Enormous effort, big declarations, extremely fast. He's in love with the pursuit and the moment he's certain of you the energy evaporates. He often doesn't understand it himself.

The cost: three weeks of the best feeling you've ever had, then six months of trying to get it back. You'll blame yourself. It was never about you.

The one who's not over her.

Still available, still warm, and there's someone in the background. Sometimes an ex, sometimes a woman who said no, sometimes just the version of his life he hasn't let go of.

The cost: you become the sensible option, and you'll know it, and you'll stay anyway because he's genuinely good company.

The one who needs managing.

His life is chaotic and you are extremely competent. Within months you're handling his admin, his moods, his family, his crises. It feels like being needed and it's exhausting.

The cost: your own pillars, one at a time, while you're too busy to notice.

And the one who's actually available.

Consistent. Clear. Slightly less exciting. Does what he says, turns up, tells you where he stands early, and is sometimes a bit boring in month one because nothing is on fire.

The cost: nothing. This is the one. And he's the one most women reject, because he produces no activation, and by now you know why.

Here's what I want you to do with this.

Don't use it to judge men. Use it to shorten your timeline.

Each of the first four reveals himself inside six weeks if you know what you're looking at. The comfortable one is vague about the future while being warm in the present. The collector front-loads everything. The one who isn't over her mentions her. The one who needs managing has a crisis in week two.

You don't need to be suspicious. You need to be literate.

What actually works.

Give it six weeks and name the pattern. Then decide, once, with the pattern in front of you.

Watch which one you're drawn to. That's the more useful information. If you've dated three collectors you have a type, and the type is about you.

And give the available one four dates before you decide he's boring.

You are not unlucky. You have been meeting the same five men your whole life and starting the investigation from scratch every time.

Your one move today.

Take your last three. Name each one.

Then look at whether there's a repeat. There almost always is.

Which one keeps finding you? Reply and tell me, this is the one I'd most like to know.
