What to do when he breadcrumbs

Stop eating the crumb.

Most women are being kept warm and calling it a relationship.

A message every few days. A story reply. A "we should do something soon" that never becomes a date. Enough to stop her moving on, never enough to be anything.

Here's what it is, mechanically.

It's minimum viable contact. He is spending the smallest possible amount to retain an option.

And it works beautifully, because of the schedule it runs on. Unpredictable, low-cost contact is the exact condition that binds hardest. He doesn't have to try. The uncertainty does the work for him.

Now, I want to be fair about something. Most men doing this are not running a strategy. He's not sitting there calculating. He likes you, he's not pursuing you, and he doesn't want to close the door. The behaviour is lazy rather than cruel.

That distinction matters for how you feel about him. It makes no difference at all to what you should do.

Here's the thing to understand about why it continues.

It continues because it's working. Every time you reply warmly to a crumb, you've confirmed that a crumb is sufficient. He's getting the warmth of your attention at the price of eleven seconds. Nobody raises their bid when the current one is being accepted.

So the crumb is not the problem. The problem is that the crumb is being paid for.

Here's how it ends, and there are only two ways.

You stop responding, and either he escalates or he disappears. Those are the only two outcomes and both of them are good.

If he escalates, you've repriced yourself and you now have an actual situation to assess. If he disappears, you've learned that there was never anything there, which you suspected, and you've got your attention back.

What there isn't, is a third option where you keep replying and he gradually becomes serious. That doesn't happen. There's no mechanism for it.

What actually works.

Match the investment exactly. He sends a one-line text after four days, you send a one-line text after four days. Not colder. Equal. Let him experience the actual temperature of what he's offering.

Never answer a vague plan. "We should do something soon" gets no response at all, or at most a warm "sounds good" with no logistics added. Do not supply the date, the time or the suggestion. If he wants to see you he can make a plan. Supplying the plan is the single most common way women keep a breadcrumb situation alive for months.

Decide your window. Four weeks. If nothing concrete happens in four weeks, it's not a slow start, it's the finished product.

And take your attention back before he's gone. You don't need him to end it for you to stop waiting.

He is not building up to something. He is spending the minimum required to keep you available, and it's working.

Your one move today.

Next crumb, don't add anything. No plan, no enthusiasm, no logistics. Match it and stop.

Watch what he does with the silence you didn't fill.

How long has your longest breadcrumb situation run? Reply with the months. No judgment, I had a nine.
