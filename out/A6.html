Why he remembers the girl who left

Unfinished things don't close.

Most women assume he's forgotten the ones who left. He chose you. You're the one who's here.

Then two years in he mentions her name in a story and something in your chest drops, because you can hear it. She's not finished.

Here's why, and it has nothing to do with how great she was.

Your brain holds open loops with far more force than closed ones. Unfinished tasks keep taking up memory. Interrupted stories keep demanding resolution. A psychologist called Zeigarnik noticed waiters remembered unpaid orders perfectly and forgot settled ones entirely, and the effect is named after her.

A woman who left is an unpaid order.

He never found out how it ends. There was no slow decline, no final boring year, no moment where it became ordinary. The file is still open and his mind keeps putting it back on top of the pile.

Meanwhile the woman who stayed through everything, who explained herself, who gave him every reassurance and every chance, closed the loop completely. He knows exactly how that story ends. It ends with her still being there.

That's not a reward for her loyalty. It's a filing decision.

Now, I'm not telling you to leave a man you love so he'll obsess over you. That's black widow behaviour and it costs you your actual life.

What I'm telling you is this.

Stop closing every loop you open.

You do it constantly without noticing. You say something intriguing and then immediately explain it. You go quiet for a day and then send a paragraph about why. You create a gap and then you rush in and fill it yourself before he's had a chance to wonder.

Every one of those is you walking over and marking your own order paid.

What actually works.

Leave the end off. Mention you had a mad weekend and then move on. If he wants it, he'll ask. If he asks, that's him investing, which is the whole point.

Don't explain your absences. "Sorry I've been quiet, work's been mad and then my sister needed me and then I was ill" is four closed loops in one message. "Hey you, how's your week been" is one open one.

And never narrate your own feelings for him in full. A man who knows exactly how much you like him has nothing left to find out.

He is not still thinking about her because she was better. He's thinking about her because he never got to the end.

Your one move today.

Say one interesting thing today and then stop talking. Don't fill the silence. Let him ask.

It'll feel rude. It isn't. It's the first time you've given him something to do.

Tell me, are you an over-explainer? Be honest. Reply, I'd genuinely like to know how many of you this lands on.

See you tomorrow!
