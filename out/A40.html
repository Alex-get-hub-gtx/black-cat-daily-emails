How to be the woman other women like

This is the highest status signal there is.

Most women treat other women as competition.

She's polite to them and warm to men. She's a bit cooler with the attractive ones. She doesn't quite trust the ones her boyfriend likes. And she has no idea what this is costing her.

Here's the mechanism.

Men assess a woman's value socially before they assess it individually, and the strongest social signal available is how other women treat her.

Think about how a man reads a room. He's not doing a detailed independent analysis of each woman. That's expensive and slow. What he does instead is read the social structure, because the social structure has already done the work.

And the thing he reads most heavily is this: who do the women orbit?

A woman that other women pull toward, defer to, light up around, is marked as high status before she has said a word to him. A woman that other women are cool toward is marked down, no matter how beautiful she is. This is stronger than looks. It is stronger than almost anything.

There's a name for the general principle, preselection. We assume something is valuable if other people have already decided it is. And a man's instinct is that women are the experts on women.

So being liked by women is not a nice side benefit of being a good person. It's the single highest leverage thing you can do for how men see you.

Here's what loses it, and it's always small things.

Being warmer to men than to women in a group. Everyone notices. It reads as the whole of your strategy in one gesture.

Not introducing women to each other. A woman who connects people is central. A woman who keeps her relationships separate is peripheral.

And the quiet one: not complimenting other women out loud. Especially the beautiful ones. Especially in front of men.

What actually works.

Compliment women in front of men, specifically and genuinely. Not on their outfit. On something that costs you something to acknowledge. "She's the smartest person I know." This does the opposite of what you fear. It raises you.

Be the one who makes introductions. Connecting two people makes you the centre of both relationships.

Give women your real attention in a mixed group. When a woman is speaking, turn toward her. Men clock this instantly and it is enormously attractive, which is a strange thing to say but it's true.

And be warm to the woman you feel threatened by. That's the whole test. That's where the status is.

He doesn't need to work out whether you're valuable. He just looks at which way the women in the room are facing.

Your one move today.

Compliment one woman today, out loud, in front of someone else, on something that isn't her appearance.

Notice what it does to the room.

Are you better with men or with women? Be honest. Reply, nobody is judging, this is learned.

See you tomorrow!
