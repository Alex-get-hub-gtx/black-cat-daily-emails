How to reset without a conversation

Changing it beats negotiating it.

You want to fix the dynamic, so you want to talk about the dynamic.

It makes complete sense. It also almost never works, and it's worth understanding why before you spend another evening on it.

Here's the mechanism.

A conversation about a pattern invites a negotiation about the pattern. A change in the pattern does not.

When you raise it, several things happen immediately. He has to defend his version. You have to justify yours. The disagreement becomes about whose account is accurate rather than about what happens next. And at the end, the best possible outcome is an agreement, which is a promise about future behaviour, which is worth almost nothing.

Meanwhile the pattern itself is untouched.

And there's a worse effect. Raising it tells him the current arrangement is bothering you, which tells him you're invested enough to be bothered, which, if he's comfortable, is reassuring rather than alarming.

Here's what a reset actually is.

You change your behaviour, without reference to his, and let the system re-equilibrate around the new input.

No announcement. No "I've been thinking". No warning shot. You simply become slightly different and he adjusts, because he has to.

Here's what that looks like concretely.

You used to reply within minutes. Now you reply when you see it. Nothing said.

You used to be free whenever. Now you have Tuesdays and Thursdays. Nothing said.

You used to drive the planning. Now you don't. If nothing gets planned, nothing happens, and you go and do something else. Nothing said.

Within about three weeks the shape of the thing is different. And it happened without a single conversation in which he got to explain why he's like this.

Here's the one exception, and it matters.

Reset without a conversation when the issue is effort, attention, availability, or dynamic. Those are all behavioural and they respond to behavioural change.

Have the conversation when the issue is factual or structural. Whether he wants children. Whether he's seeing other people. Whether he intends to move. Those are not patterns, they're facts, and facts require asking.

Don't use behavioural resets to avoid asking a question you need answered. And don't use conversations to avoid changing something you could just change.

What actually works.

Pick two behaviours and change them this week. Quietly.

Say nothing about it, including if he comments. You already have the four words for that.

Give it three weeks before you assess.

And if he asks what's going on, "nothing, why?" is a complete and honest answer, because from your side nothing is going on. You're just living slightly differently.

You have had the conversation four times. Try the fortnight where you change one thing and say nothing, and watch how much faster it moves.

Your one move today.

Choose two behaviours. Write them down. Start today.

Reply response time and planning are the two that move things fastest.
