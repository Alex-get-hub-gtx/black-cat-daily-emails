Why he keeps you but will not commit

He's already got it all.

Most women decide he has a problem with commitment.

He's scared. He's got baggage. His last one hurt him. She builds a whole explanation, and the explanation lets her keep waiting.

Here's the actual reason, and it's not comfortable.

He already has everything commitment would give him. So commitment costs him something and gains him nothing.

Look at the ledger from his side.

He has your time. Your affection. Your body. Your loyalty. He has someone who checks in on him, who's there on a bad week, who tells her friends about him. He has the girlfriend experience in full.

Now, what does putting a label on it add for him?

Nothing. It subtracts. It adds obligation, visibility, the loss of his options, a conversation with his friends, and a cost if it ends.

So he's not confused and he's not frightened. He's rational. He's at a comfort ceiling. He's got the top of what's available at the price he's currently paying, and no sane person pays more for what they already have.

This is why "we need to talk about where this is going" never works. You're asking him to volunteer for a price increase on a product he already owns.

Here's what the waiting actually looks like from the outside.

Eighteen months. No label. She's met two of his friends, by accident. Every few months she raises it, he says he's just not someone who rushes, she accepts it because he's lovely when he's there.

And she's not wrong, he is lovely when he's there. That's exactly the trap. The arrangement is genuinely pleasant. That's why it's lasted eighteen months.

What actually works.

Change what's available, not what's discussed. The ceiling moves when the inputs move. Less access, less of the girlfriend experience, more of your own life. Not announced. Just done.

Let him feel the gap before you explain the gap. If you explain it, it becomes a negotiation, and he will negotiate. If you simply have less to give, he has to respond to a reality instead of a request.

And set your deadline privately. Decide the date in your own head. Tell nobody, least of all him. Then honour it.

A man will not pay for what he already has. He will only pay to stop losing it.

Your one move today.

Pick one piece of the girlfriend experience he's getting for free and quietly stop supplying it this week. The good morning text. The Sunday availability. The emotional support call.

Say nothing. Change nothing else. Just let the supply drop.

How long have you been waiting for a label? Reply with the number of months. You might be surprised how saying it out loud feels.
