Why he got bored of the perfect girlfriend

Perfect is a flat line.

Most women think that if they can just be flawless, he'll have no reason to leave.

So they become unobjectionable. Always kind. Always supportive. Never difficult. The girlfriend you could not write a complaint about.

And then he leaves anyway, or worse, he stays and goes quiet, and she's left holding a list of everything she did right.

Here's what happened.

Your brain does not respond to how good something is. It responds to change.

The chemical everyone calls the pleasure chemical, dopamine, isn't really about pleasure. It fires on prediction error. On the gap between what your brain expected and what actually happened. When reality beats the forecast, you get a hit. When reality matches the forecast exactly, you get almost nothing.

So a woman who is reliably wonderful becomes, over time, a forecast. And a forecast produces no signal at all.

This is why he can genuinely love you and feel nothing. The love is real. The signal is gone.

And here's the cruel part. The harder you worked at being perfect, the faster you flattened the line. Because perfection is, by definition, predictable.

Picture the first three months. You were a bit of a mystery. He didn't know if you'd be free. He didn't know what you'd say. Every single interaction carried a small charge of not knowing.

Then you got comfortable. You started confirming plans in advance. Replying straight away. Being reachable, reliable, consistent, all the things you were told to be.

And you did not become less attractive. You became less surprising. Which, to his nervous system, is the same thing.

What actually works.

Stop optimising for approval and start allowing variance. Have opinions he doesn't share. Change your mind. Be unavailable because something better came up, and don't apologise for it.

Keep one thing he doesn't have access to. A friendship, a project, a part of your week. Not a secret. Just a room he can't walk into whenever he likes.

And let him wonder, occasionally, what you thought of that. Not everything you feel needs to be reported.

He didn't stop wanting you because you weren't enough. He stopped because there was nothing left to find out.

Your one move today.

Say one true thing you'd normally soften. A film you actually hated. A plan you don't actually want to go to. Something small where your real answer differs from your agreeable answer.

You'll feel the air change. That's the line going spiky again.
