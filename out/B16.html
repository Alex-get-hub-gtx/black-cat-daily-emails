How to be chased inside a relationship

The chase does not have to end.

Everyone treats the chase as a phase. He pursues, he catches, it's over, and what's left is companionship.

It isn't a phase. It's a condition, and the condition can be maintained. It just requires something most couples remove on purpose.

Here's the mechanism.

Desire requires a gap. Certainty closes it.

In the beginning there's a gap by default. He doesn't know how you feel, where this is going, what you're doing on Saturday, or what you think of him. Every one of those is an open question, and open questions generate approach behaviour.

Then the relationship does what relationships do. It resolves them. On purpose, because resolving uncertainty is what makes someone feel secure, and security is genuinely the point.

The problem is that the same resolution that produces security also removes every single gap. And a man with no gaps has nothing to move toward.

This is why good relationships go flat. Not because the love faded. Because the uncertainty was successfully eliminated, which was the stated goal.

So the question is how to be fully committed and still have gaps.

And the answer is not manufactured uncertainty. Don't be vague about your feelings, don't withhold affection, don't play games with a man you're building something with. That's the widow move and in a relationship it reads as cruelty.

The answer is that the gaps move from the relationship to you.

Here's what that means practically.

Certainty about the relationship: total. He should never wonder whether you love him or whether you're leaving. That's the foundation and it should be unshakeable.

Uncertainty about you: permanent. What you're thinking, what you'll say, what you've been up to, what you're becoming, what you'll decide to do next year. Those stay live.

A woman who is completely committed and completely unfinished is the thing that never gets old.

What actually works.

Keep growing in a direction he isn't managing. A new skill, a career move, a sport, a subject. The specifics don't matter. The movement does. A man cannot get bored of a woman who is becoming someone slightly different every six months.

Keep some of your inner world unreported. Same ten percent as before. In a long relationship this matters more, not less.

Keep going out without him. Your friends, your plans, your Thursday. The most attractive thing in a long relationship is a woman who still has her own evening.

And stop pre-emptively resolving his questions. If he asks what you're up to on Saturday, you don't have to deliver the full itinerary. "Something with Sophie" is a complete answer.

He does not stop chasing because he caught you. He stops because you handed him every remaining answer in the first year.

Your one move today.

Name one thing you're going to get better at this year that has nothing to do with him.

Then start it this week, and don't make it a conversation.

What's gone flat for you, the relationship or you? Reply honestly.
