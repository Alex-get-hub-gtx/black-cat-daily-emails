Why you need your own money

It changes your voice.

Most women think money is the unromantic part of this conversation.

She doesn't want to think about it. It feels transactional, cold, a bit grim. She'd rather talk about connection.

Here's why I'm going to talk about it anyway.

Money is not about money. It's about the exit you can afford, and the exit you can afford changes how you speak.

I want you to notice something about yourself. The way you raise a problem with a man when you could leave tomorrow is completely different from the way you raise it when you couldn't.

When leaving is survivable, you say the thing once, plainly, and you mean it. Your voice is level. You're not building a case. You're informing him.

When leaving isn't survivable, you hint. You soften. You raise it and then retreat. You find yourself apologising halfway through your own sentence. And he can hear all of it, because the dependency is in the sound of your voice, not in anything you said.

You cannot fake that. No amount of boundary scripts will make a dependent woman sound independent. The sound comes from the arithmetic.

Here's the part that matters, and it's smaller than you think.

You don't need to be rich. You need three months.

Three months of your own costs, in your own account, in your own name. That's it. That's the threshold where leaving goes from impossible to merely difficult, and the change in how you carry yourself is immediate and obvious.

I've watched women change more in the six months of building that than in two years of therapy, because therapy was trying to give her a feeling that the arithmetic wouldn't allow.

And here's the cruel version. A man who is comfortable with you being financially dependent on him, who likes it, who discourages you from earning or saving, has told you exactly what he intends to do with that leverage. Not all of them do. The ones who do, do it early, and it looks like generosity.

What actually works.

Separate account, your name only, and something automatic going into it. The amount is less important than the automation. Twenty pounds a week is a start and it's also a declaration.

Know your actual monthly number. Most women don't. Write down what it costs to be you for a month. Then you know what three months looks like and it stops being abstract.

Never let your income go to zero inside a relationship. Reduce it if you want. Not to zero. Zero is the one that cannot be undone quickly.

And keep it quiet. This is yours. It is not a threat, it is not a conversation, it is not something you mention. It's just true.

Your standards are only as real as your ability to walk away from a man who ignores them.

Your one move today.

Open the separate account. Today. It takes about four minutes on your phone.

Put ten pounds in it. The amount is not the point.

Do you have your own account? Reply yes or no, I'd honestly like to know the split.

See you tomorrow!
