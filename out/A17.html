What he means by "I'm not ready"

He's not talking about relationships.

Most women hear "I'm not ready for a relationship" and believe the word relationship.

So she waits. She gives him space to get ready. She tells herself the timing is wrong, not the man.

Here's what he almost always means.

He is not uncertain about relationships. He is uncertain about this one.

Men are not generally confused about whether they want a woman. Certainty is fast in men, much faster than women expect. He usually knows within weeks, sometimes within days, whether this is a woman he'd rearrange his life for.

What he's uncertain about is whether he's willing to pay for this. And "I'm not ready" is the kindest available sentence for that, because the honest version is unsayable.

The proof is everywhere once you see it. Watch what happens when the man who wasn't ready meets someone six months later and is engaged within a year. He didn't become ready. He became certain.

Now, there is a real version of this. Occasionally a man genuinely has no capacity. He's grieving, he's in the middle of a divorce, his business is collapsing. That man exists and he's telling the truth.

Here's how you tell the difference, and it takes about two weeks.

A man with no capacity but real certainty keeps showing up. He'll say he can't offer much right now and then he'll still be there on Thursday. He'll be clear about what he can't do and consistent about what he can. His effort doesn't drop, his availability does.

A man with capacity but no certainty does the opposite. He keeps the access and drops the effort. He's not ready for a relationship but he's ready for your Saturday, your body and your emotional support.

One of those is honest. The other one is a discount.

What actually works.

Believe behaviour, not the sentence. Give it two weeks and watch which of the two patterns above you're in. You will know.

Take the sentence at face value, exactly. If he's not ready for a relationship then he doesn't get the relationship. Not the daily texts, not the Sunday, not the girlfriend labour. He gets what someone who isn't ready gets.

And do not help him get ready. You cannot build certainty in someone about yourself. Every week you spend trying is a week you spent auditioning.

A man who is not ready for you will somehow become extremely ready for someone else. That was never about timing.

Your one move today.

If you've heard that sentence, take him at his word today. One thing you've been providing that a not-ready man shouldn't get, stop providing it.

No announcement. Just alignment.
