First date mistakes that decide everything

Three of them are irreversible.

Most women think a first date is a chance to show him who she is.

So she's open. She's honest. She tells him about her last relationship, she asks what he's looking for, and she insists on paying her half to show she's not that kind of woman.

Every one of those is a mistake, and all three of them are hard to come back from.

Here's why, one at a time.

The ex.

Never mention him. Not the good version, not the bad version, not the funny anecdote. Here's the mechanism: whatever you say about your ex becomes what he expects to be said about him. Talk about how badly you were treated and you've shown him the role available in your story. Talk warmly and he's in competition with a ghost. There is no version of this that helps you.

And there's a second cost. Talking about your last relationship on a first date tells him where your attention is. He's not jealous, he's just filed you as unfinished.

"Where is this going?"

Not on a first date, not in the first month. I know the impulse. She wants to be efficient, she doesn't want to waste time, so she asks early.

The mechanism: a man cannot answer that question honestly before he's invested, and asking it forces him to produce a sentence instead of a decision. Whatever he says will be reassuring and worthless. And it tells him you need certainty, which tells him your centre of gravity is outside yourself.

Efficiency is the wrong goal here. Information is the goal, and information comes from watching, not asking.

The bill.

Let him pay the first one. I don't care how modern you are.

The mechanism is investment. A man who has paid has invested, and investment is the beginning of value. A man who has paid half has participated in a transaction with a stranger. The amount is irrelevant. It's about who did a thing for whom.

And the way you do it matters. Don't reach. Don't offer. Don't do the wallet gesture. Just let it happen, and then say thank you, once, warmly, and move on. If he doesn't pay, that's information too, and it's the most useful thing you'll learn all evening.

What actually works instead.

Ask about him and go one layer deeper than the answer. Not interrogation, curiosity. "Why that?" is the best question in dating.

Be slightly mysterious by accident, not on purpose. You don't have to withhold. You just don't have to volunteer your entire history in ninety minutes.

And leave first. Yes, even if it's going well. Especially then.

A first date is not an interview where you present your case. It's two hours in which you find out what he does when nobody is making him do anything.

Your one move today.

If you've got a date coming up, decide now: no ex, no future, no wallet.

Three rules. That's the whole preparation.

Which of the three have you broken? Reply and tell me, I have broken all three in one evening.
