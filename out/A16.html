Why he was obsessed for three weeks

Then it just stopped.

Most women have lived this exact story.

Three weeks of absolute intensity. Good morning texts, plans being made, him telling his friends. She has never felt so wanted.

Then week four, and the temperature drops, and nothing happened. Nothing happened. That's what makes it so disorientating.

Here's what happened.

Novelty expired. And nothing had been built to replace it.

The first three weeks of attraction run almost entirely on novelty. New person, new information, unpredictable outcomes. That's a dopamine environment and it is intense, and it is also, biologically, temporary. It is not designed to last. It burns out in roughly two to six weeks on its own.

Which means everything you felt in those three weeks was real and none of it was evidence. It was the fuel that was always going to run out.

The question that decides everything is what's underneath when it does.

If what's underneath is genuine attachment, real investment, a man who's put effort in and has something to lose, then week four is where it gets good. The intensity drops and depth replaces it.

If what's underneath is nothing, week four is where it ends. And it will feel like he changed, when actually he just ran out of petrol.

Here's the mistake almost every woman makes at week four.

She feels the drop and she tries to recreate the first three weeks. More enthusiasm, more availability, more texts, more effort. She tries to manufacture novelty by giving more of herself.

It cannot work. You cannot be new again. The only thing you can add is depth, and depth is built through his investment, not through your effort.

What actually works.

Stop spending novelty so fast in the first three weeks. Don't tell him everything. Don't be available every night. The woman who gives all her novelty away in twenty days has nothing left at day thirty. Ration it.

Build investment while the novelty is still running. Those three weeks are when he's most willing to make effort, so that's when you let him. Let him drive, plan, pay, show up. You're building the floor that catches you at week four.

And when the drop comes, do not chase the peak. Go quieter, not louder. Let him notice the absence of something he'd got used to.

The three weeks were never the relationship. They were the window in which the relationship was either built or not.

Your one move today.

If you're in week two or three with someone right now, do one thing: hold something back today. One story, one night, one piece of yourself.

You'll need it at week five.

How many three week stories do you have? Reply with the number, I promise you are not the only one in double figures.
