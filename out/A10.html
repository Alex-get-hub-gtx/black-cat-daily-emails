He does not want a challenge

Stop playing hard to get.

Most women hear everything I say and translate it into one thing: play hard to get.

So they do. They wait exactly forty minutes before replying. They say they're busy when they're on the sofa. They do the thing they read about and it goes wrong, and then they decide the whole approach doesn't work.

Here's why it went wrong.

Men do not want a challenge. They want a reason.

A challenge is an obstacle between him and a thing. Obstacles are annoying. Men do not fall in love with obstacles, they route around them or they give up, and either way the woman becomes a problem rather than a prize.

A reason is different. A reason is why you're worth the effort in the first place.

A woman who is hard to get for no reason is just difficult. A woman who is hard to get because her life is genuinely full and her standards are genuinely real is not an obstacle. She's a reason.

The behaviour looks identical from the outside. She's not free on Thursday. But the engine underneath is completely different, and he can feel which one it is within about two interactions.

Here's the test I'd want you to run on yourself.

When you don't reply for two hours, what are you doing?

If the answer is "staring at my phone, timing it, feeling slightly sick" then you're playing hard to get, and he will sense the desperation coming through the delay. Strategic unavailability from an anxious woman reads as anxious. The delay doesn't hide it, it broadcasts it.

If the answer is "I was at pilates and then I got coffee with Sophie and I genuinely forgot" then you're unavailable. And unavailable is the thing that works.

You cannot fake this from the outside in. It has to be structurally true.

What actually works.

Build the reason, then the behaviour takes care of itself. Fill the week. Have the friends, the project, the gym, the thing you're learning. Then you don't have to remember to be busy, you'll just be busy.

Never perform a delay. If you're free and you want to text him, text him. The warmth is not the problem. Availability with nothing behind it is the problem.

And let your standards be about you, not about him. "I don't do last minute" is a standard. "I'm not replying to teach him a lesson" is a tactic, and tactics have a smell.

He is not looking for a woman who's difficult to reach. He's looking for a woman whose life was clearly worth reaching into.

Your one move today.

Put one real thing in your diary for this week that has nothing to do with any man. Then, when he asks about that night, tell the truth: you've got something on.

No games required. Just a life.

Be honest with me, have you ever sat on a reply and watched the clock? Reply and tell me, there is no judgment here, I have done it too.

See you tomorrow!
