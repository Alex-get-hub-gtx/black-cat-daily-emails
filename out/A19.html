Why he is different around his friends

That's the honest version.

Most women notice this and talk themselves out of it.

In private he's affectionate, open, lovely. Around his friends he's cooler. A bit jokey at her expense. A little distance that wasn't there in the kitchen that morning.

She tells herself men are just like that in groups.

Here's the thing. How a man treats you in front of other men is his honest rating of you.

Private behaviour is cheap. Nobody is watching, nothing is at stake, and he gets everything he wants from being warm. Public behaviour has a cost. His standing among men is the single thing most men are most careful with, and he will not spend it on a woman he doesn't rate.

So when he becomes cooler in front of them, he's protecting his position. Which tells you his position would be damaged by being seen to be devoted to you. Which tells you exactly where you sit.

And when he's warmer in front of them, when he pulls you in, says your name proudly, lets them see him look after you, that is the most expensive signal a man can send. He's spending status on you in front of the people whose opinion he actually cares about.

That's the signal. Not the affection in private. The affection in public.

Here's the version I see most.

They've been together seven months. At home it's wonderful. At his friend's birthday she's standing slightly outside the circle, and he doesn't notice for forty minutes, and when he does he says something slightly dismissive about her that gets a laugh.

She spends the drive home deciding not to mention it.

That night was not a bad night. That night was the actual information, and she chose the kitchen version instead.

What actually works.

Weight public behaviour far above private behaviour. When they conflict, public wins. Always.

Don't raise it in the moment, and don't raise it as a complaint later. Instead, do the only thing that's genuinely powerful: become someone whose approval his friends can see he has. Be warm, be interesting, be slightly unbothered by him in front of them. Men recalibrate fast when their friends like a woman more than they expected.

And if he makes you the joke, don't absorb it with a smile. One beat of silence and a level look does more than any conversation. Men feel that in a group. Everybody feels that in a group.

He can be in love with you in the kitchen and still be telling you the truth in the pub.

Your one move today.

Think about the last time you were around his friends. Not how you felt. What he did. Did he move toward you or away?

You already know the answer. Let yourself have it.

Have you ever had the kitchen version and the pub version? Reply, I want to know how common this is.
