What to watch in the first ninety days

It's all visible early.

Almost everything you'll eventually find out is visible in the first ninety days. It's just that nobody's looking, because the first ninety days is when you're busy being delighted.

Here's what's observable early and never changes.

His pace. How fast he moves at the start tells you about his pattern, not about his feelings. Very fast is usually about him. A man who is intense in week two is running something that isn't about you yet, because he doesn't know you yet.

How he handles a no. Any no, however small. Can't do Thursday. Don't want to go there. Not tonight. His response to the first small no is his response to every future one, and you'll see it in week three if you give him the chance.

Whether plans survive contact with reality. Not whether he makes them. Whether they hold. A man who proposes lovely things that keep evaporating has shown you the whole thing.

How he is when you're unavailable. The first time you're genuinely busy for a few days. Does he get interesting, or does he get resentful, or does he vanish? This is enormously informative and it happens naturally if you have a life.

His honesty about small things. Does he sand off details to look slightly better? Small dishonesty is not a small trait.

What he's like tired or hungry or stressed. The first time you see him in a bad state is the first time you see him. Everything before that was the presentation.

Whether he asks you anything. Really asks. Some men never do, and it's visible by week four if you count.

And whether he's curious about your life or just available for it.

Here's the discipline, and it's the hard part.

Watching is not the same as testing. You're not going to manufacture these. You're going to live normally and notice what happens, because all of them occur naturally if you have a full enough life.

The woman who manufactures a test gets a performance. The woman who is simply busy gets the truth.

What actually works.

Keep a short private note for the first ninety days. One line a week, facts only. Your memory will revise everything after month four.

Pay attention at the first small friction, not at the big moments.

And resist deciding in the first six weeks. Both directions. The glow distorts upward and the occasional red flag distorts downward, and ninety days is about when the picture becomes real.

None of this is hidden. It's just that the first ninety days is when you're least inclined to look, and it's the only period when looking is easy.

Your one move today.

If you're early with someone, start the note today. One line a week.

In three months it'll be the most valuable thing you own.
