How to calm down without your phone

Ninety seconds, four inputs.

Most women try to calm down by getting information.

Check if he's replied. Check if he's online. Read the conversation again. Look at her profile one more time. Get the one piece of data that will settle the feeling.

It never settles it. It cannot, and here's why.

The feeling is not in your mind. It's in your body. And information cannot reach it.

When you're activated, the signal is coming from below the thinking part of your brain. Those pathways run upward much more heavily than they run downward, which is a tidy way of saying your body talks to your mind far more than your mind talks to your body.

Which is why reasoning with yourself doesn't work. You're shouting down a one way street.

So you have to go in through the body. There are four inputs that actually work and they all work in under two minutes.

The long exhale. Four counts in through your nose, eight counts out through your mouth. The exhale is the part that matters, because that's the half of the breath cycle wired to the brake. Five rounds. You'll feel it on the third.

Cold. Cold water on your face and wrists, or a cold shower if you're really gone. The cold triggers a reflex that drops heart rate directly. This is the fastest one of the four, and the most unpleasant, which is a fair trade at midnight.

Pressure and ground. Feet flat on a hard floor, preferably cold. Then something with weight on you, a blanket, a cushion against your chest, or just your own hand pressed firmly on your sternum. Deep pressure calms the system. It's why weighted blankets work.

Movement. Not a walk. Something that burns the adrenaline off. Twenty squats, run up the stairs twice, shake your hands out hard for thirty seconds. Adrenaline is fuel and it needs to be spent, not reasoned with.

Here's what I want you to notice about all four.

None of them require him to do anything. None of them require you to know anything. And none of them can be taken away from you.

That's the whole point. You are building the ability to regulate your own state, which is the one thing that makes everything else in my work possible. You cannot be unbothered while you are dependent on his reply to settle down.

What actually works, in order.

Pick two of the four and commit to them. Not all four, two. The long exhale plus one other.

Do them before the phone, not instead of it. You'll still check. Just check afterwards, from a settled body. You'll be astonished how different the message looks.

The black cat is not a woman who never gets activated. She's a woman who can bring herself down without anyone's help.

Your one move today.

Four in, eight out. Five rounds. Right now, before you do anything else.

That's your baseline. Everything I teach sits on top of it.
