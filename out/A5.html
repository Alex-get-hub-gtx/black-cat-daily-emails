Men bond through effort, not affection

Nothing free ever gets valued.

Most women believe that if they make a man's life easier, he'll want to keep them in it.

It's such a reasonable belief. It's also backwards.

Here's the mechanism, and it's one of the best established things in psychology.

People do not value what they receive. They value what they paid for.

When you work for something, your mind has a problem. It has spent real effort, and effort has to be justified. So it adjusts the value of the thing upward until the cost makes sense. This is why people love the wobbly furniture they built themselves and shrug at the perfect thing that arrived in a box.

Now apply that to you.

If a man has never had to rearrange a single thing to have you, his mind has nothing to justify. You're the thing that arrived in the box. And no amount of you being wonderful can retroactively create a cost he never paid.

This is why the kindest women get taken for granted by men who are genuinely nice. He's not a villain. He simply has no internal record of having worked for her.

Here's what it looks like in practice.

She's proud of being easy to be with. She drives to him because his place is nicer. She says "whatever you want" about dinner. She tells him not to worry about her birthday. She's doing all of this out of love, and she's building him a relationship that costs him nothing to be in and therefore nothing to lose.

Then he gets an opportunity in another city, and he takes it, and she cannot understand how he could walk away from something so good.

He didn't walk away from something good. He walked away from something free.

What actually works.

Let there be friction. Not drama, friction. Your place is across town. You're not free on Wednesday. You'd love to but you've got a thing. These are not games, they're the actual shape of a woman with a life.

Let him solve things. When something is slightly inconvenient, don't fix it before he notices. Give him the three seconds to step in.

And stop pre-paying. Don't do the lovely thing before he's done anything. Generosity in the wrong order reads as an application, not as affection.

Nothing free goes on the mantelpiece. It goes in the drawer with everything else that was always going to be there.

Your one move today.

There's one thing you do for him that he's never asked for and never noticed. Stop doing it. Just this week. Say nothing.

Either he notices, which tells you something. Or he doesn't, which tells you more.

What's the thing you do that he's never once said thank you for? Reply and tell me, I have a feeling I know the answer.

See you tomorrow!
