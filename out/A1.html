Here's what men actually want

It's not what you've been told.

Most women think men want a pretty, agreeable, low maintenance woman.

So that's what they become. They get their lashes done, they keep the peace, they stay easy. And then they watch a man commit to a woman who is none of those things.

Here's what's actually going on.

A man does not choose a woman based on how she makes his life easier. He chooses based on how he feels about himself when he's around her.

Read that again, because it's the whole thing.

Every man is walking around with a quiet running assessment of himself. Am I capable. Am I respected. Am I the kind of man who gets to have something good. And the woman who makes that assessment go up is the woman he cannot stop thinking about.

That's not romance. That's architecture.

So when you make everything easy for him, you're not giving him what he wants. You're removing the thing he needs. He needs to feel like he earned you. If he didn't earn you, then having you proves nothing about him, and a man cannot stay interested in something that proves nothing.

This is why the "low maintenance" woman gets taken for granted. Not because she's unlovable. Because she's free. And nothing free goes on the mantelpiece.

Think about the last time a man was genuinely obsessed with you. Not a man you were chasing. A man who was chasing you.

I'd put money on this: you weren't trying very hard. You were busy. You were maybe a bit indifferent. You had your own thing going on and he had to work his way into it.

That wasn't an accident.

What actually works is this.

Stop managing his comfort. Let him be slightly uncertain. Not anxious, uncertain. There's a difference and it's the difference between a man who pursues and a man who panics.

Let him do things for you. Small things, immediately. A man who has invested nothing in you has nothing to lose. A man who has carried your bags, booked the table, driven across town, has started building something and he will defend it.

And then, this is the part nobody does, let him see you be respected by other people. Your friends, your colleagues, the waiter who knows your name. Men read status socially before they read it individually.

A man doesn't fall in love with the woman who makes everything easy. He falls in love with the woman who makes him feel like a man who earned something rare.

Your one move today.

The next time he offers to do something for you, say yes. Don't say "no it's fine, I've got it." Don't minimise it. Just say "that would be lovely, thank you" and let him.

That's it. One yes to his effort instead of one more proof that you don't need anything.

I'm curious, what's the one thing you always say no to because you don't want to be a bother? Reply and tell me, I read every one.

See you tomorrow!
