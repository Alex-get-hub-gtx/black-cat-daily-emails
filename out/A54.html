Green flags nobody talks about

None of them look romantic.

Most women are looking for the wrong green flags.

He texts back quickly. He's affectionate. He said he's looking for something serious. He's lovely to her.

None of those predict anything. All of them are available to any man on his best behaviour for eight weeks.

Here are six that actually predict a man who stays. None of them are romantic and that's precisely why they work.

He has long friendships.

Men who have kept the same three or four friends for ten or fifteen years know how to maintain something past the exciting part. Men whose friendships are all under two years old have a pattern, and you will eventually be in it.

He tells you something that makes him look bad.

Unprompted, early, with no spin on it. A time he behaved badly, a thing he's still working on, a failure he hasn't dressed up. This is genuinely rare and it's the single best signal on this list, because it costs him something to say and it means he's not managing an image.

He's the same with the waiter.

Said this before and it stays true. How a man treats someone who cannot affect his life is who he is in year five, when you also cannot affect his life very much.

He does what he said he'd do about something tiny.

Not the big gestures. The small logistics. He said he'd send the link, and the link arrived. He said Tuesday and it was Tuesday. Reliability at the trivial scale is the only honest predictor of reliability at the serious scale, and most people never check it.

He can be disagreed with.

Have a real difference of opinion early about something that doesn't matter, and watch him. Does he get curious, or does he get cold? A man who cannot tolerate being disagreed with about a film cannot tolerate being disagreed with about anything.

He has something he's building.

Work, a craft, a project, a goal. Not necessarily successful. Ongoing. A man with nothing he's pulling toward will eventually make you the thing, and that is not a compliment, it's a weight.

Here's what I want you to notice about the whole list.

Not one of them is about how he treats you.

That's deliberate. How he treats you in the first three months is the least reliable data available, because that's the window where every man is at his best. Everything above is about how he treats his life, which is the thing that doesn't change.

What actually works.

Check the small promises. That's your fastest test and it takes two weeks.

Create one small disagreement on purpose and watch what happens. This is the most useful ninety seconds of the first month.

Look at the age of his friendships, not the warmth of his words.

The best predictor of how a man will treat you in year three is not how he treats you now. It's how he treats everything else.

Your one move today.

Pick the small promises one. Next time he says he'll do a small thing, notice whether he does it.

That's the whole test. It costs you nothing and it's remarkably accurate.

Which of the six would your last one have failed? Reply, I'd like to know which is most common.
