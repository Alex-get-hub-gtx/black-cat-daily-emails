The chemical that makes a man commit

It isn't love and it isn't sex.

Most women think a man commits when he loves you enough.

So they focus on being loved. Being adored, being appreciated, being told she's the one. And then they're genuinely shocked when a man who clearly adores them will not commit to anything.

Love and commitment run on different systems. You can max out one and get nothing from the other.

Here's the part nobody explains.

Attachment in men runs heavily on vasopressin. It's a hormone, close cousin to oxytocin, and it's the one associated with pair bonding, territory and protectiveness. It's a big part of why a man goes from enjoying a woman to considering her his.

And here's what builds it. Not comfort. Repetition plus effort.

Vasopressin pathways strengthen through repeated investment in a specific person. He does something for you, his system registers the cost, and the cost gets filed against your name. Do that enough times and you are not a woman he's seeing. You're his.

Which means the woman who requires nothing of a man is, biologically, the woman he will never bond to. She hasn't given his system anything to file.

I've watched this a hundred times.

Two women, same man, same six months. The first one makes it effortless. She drives to him, she pays her half, she never asks for anything, she's proud of being no trouble. He likes her enormously. He will not define it.

The second one lets him come to her. Lets him book the restaurant, carry the bag, sort the problem out. She's warm about it and she never over-thanks him. Three months in, he's telling his friends about her.

The first woman was kinder. The second woman gave his nervous system something to attach to.

What actually works.

Let him invest in small, repeated, visible ways. Repetition matters more than size. Ten small efforts beat one grand gesture, every time.

Receive it cleanly. The fastest way to kill a man's investment is to over-thank it or refuse it. "Thank you, that was perfect" and then move on. Gushing makes it feel like charity. Refusing makes it feel pointless.

And never compete with him on provision. If he's reaching for the bill, let him reach. If he's carrying something, let him carry it. Every time you jump in to prove you don't need him, you delete a deposit.

A man does not bond to the woman who needs nothing from him. He bonds to the woman he has already spent himself on.

Your one move today.

Ask him for one small thing you could easily do yourself. Not a test. Just a real small request. Then say thank you once, warmly, and change the subject.

Watch what it does to him. It's not what you expect.

Tell me, do you find it harder to ask for something or to accept it when it's offered? Reply, this one splits the room every time.
