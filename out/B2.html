Why it worked for three weeks

There's a relapse point.

It worked. That's the frustrating part.

You pulled your energy back, you got busy, you stopped answering instantly. And he responded. He started asking. He made a plan without being prompted. For about three weeks you had the thing.

And then it slid back, and you can't point at the day it happened.

Here's what you've run into, and almost nobody warns you about it.

Reconditioning has a relapse point, and it lands around week three.

Here's the mechanism. For the first two or three weeks, the change is novel to both of you. He's noticed something is different and he's responding to the difference. You're running on the energy of doing something new, which is its own kind of fuel.

Then two things happen at once.

He adapts. The new level becomes the normal level. The thing that got his attention was the change, not the state, and once the state is stable there's no change left to notice. Same mechanism as the flat line.

And you relax. This is the bigger one. It's working, he's being lovely, and the discipline that produced it starts to feel unnecessary. You reply a bit faster. You say yes to the Thursday. You give back one small thing, and then another, and none of them feel like anything.

Three weeks of progress unwinds in about nine days, and it unwinds through behaviours so small you'd struggle to name them.

Here's what week three actually requires.

Not more distance. Not another round of pulling back. If you escalate at week three you tip from black cat into black widow and he'll read it as punishment, which is a different thing entirely and it ends badly.

What it requires is holding the level you already set.

That's it. Same availability, same pace, same full week. The work at week three is not to do more. It's to not quietly give it all back because things are going well.

What actually works.

Write down what you changed, while you're doing it. Specific behaviours. "I don't reply after 10pm." "Thursday is mine." Then at week three, read the list and check yourself against it honestly.

Expect the softening. It isn't weakness, it's what happens when the threat passes. Knowing it's coming is most of the defence.

And put the structural things in place rather than relying on willpower. A booked Thursday holds itself. A decision to be less available does not.

The pull back didn't stop working at week three. You stopped doing it, one small kindness at a time.

Your one move today.

Write the list. Three to five specific behaviours you changed.

Put a reminder in your phone for twenty one days from now that just says: read the list.
