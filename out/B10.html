The consequence ladder

There are four rungs, not two.

Most women have exactly two settings.

Mention it. And leave.

So she mentions it, repeatedly, for fourteen months. Nothing changes, because mentioning is not a consequence. And then one day she hits her limit and leaves, and it looks sudden and dramatic to him, and he says he had no idea it was that serious.

He's often telling the truth. From where he was standing, nothing ever happened.

Here's the ladder. Four rungs between saying something and leaving, and most women have never used the middle two.

Rung one. You say it. Once, plainly, no emotion, no build-up. "I don't want to be the one organising everything."

That's not a consequence, it's information. But it has to be said once, clearly, so that what follows is not a mystery.

Rung two. You stop supplying it. He doesn't organise anything, so nothing happens. You don't organise it for him. You don't rescue the weekend. You go and do your own thing and the empty space stays empty.

This is the rung almost nobody uses and it's the most powerful one. It's not punishment, it's withdrawal of your labour from a thing he isn't contributing to.

Rung three. The thing gets visibly repriced. Less access, less of your time, less of the girlfriend experience, and you don't explain the connection. He experiences a change in what's available. He is not told why, because he's already been told once, at rung one.

Rung four. You go.

Here's what the ladder does that mentioning doesn't.

It gives him real information in a language he actually processes, which is behaviour. Men do not generally update from conversation. They update from changed conditions.

And it gives you something to do other than tolerate or detonate. Most women are stuck oscillating between those two, and the oscillation is exhausting and reads as instability.

Here's the discipline.

One rung at a time, and give each one real time. Two to three weeks per rung. You are not trying to shock him, you're letting a new reality establish itself.

And never announce which rung you're on. "If this doesn't change I'm going to start pulling back" is you handing him the whole map and converting a consequence into a threat.

What actually works.

Say it once at rung one. Properly, clearly, no heat.

Then stop talking and start moving up the rungs.

Expect the change to happen at rung two or three. That's where it lands if it's going to.

And if you reach rung four, you'll arrive calm, because you'll have given it a fair run and you'll know it.

You have been doing rung one for a year and calling it trying. He has experienced nothing but conversation, and conversation costs him nothing at all.

Your one move today.

Which rung are you on? Be honest.

If it's rung one and it's been months, you already know where you need to go next.

What have you said more than three times with nothing changing? Reply and tell me.
