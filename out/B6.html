Detachment is not the goal

Numb is not the same as regulated.

Somewhere in all of this a lot of women take a wrong turn.

They hear "don't be anxious" and "don't react" and "stop needing his reassurance", and they conclude the target is to stop feeling anything.

So they build a wall. And it works, in the sense that the anxiety stops. And then they wonder why nothing good is happening either.

Here's the distinction.

Regulated means you feel it and it doesn't run you. Numb means you've stopped feeling it. Those are completely different states and only one of them is available for love.

Regulation is an active skill. The feeling arrives, your body handles it, and you choose what to do. You still want him. You still feel the drop when he's distant. You just aren't operated by it.

Numbness is a shutdown. It's cheaper and faster and it genuinely does stop the pain. It also stops everything else, because the system that produces longing is the same system that produces attachment.

And here's the cost nobody mentions. A numb woman cannot be pursued successfully. A man can do everything right and get nothing back, because there's nobody home to receive it. He'll eventually stop, and she'll read that as confirmation that men leave.

How to tell which one you're in.

Regulated: you feel the pull toward your phone and you don't pick it up. There's a pull. You're aware of it. You simply have other things to do.

Numb: there's no pull at all, and there's also no warmth, and good news about him produces roughly nothing. You've described yourself recently as not really caring any more and it didn't feel like freedom, it felt like fog.

The honest test is whether you can still be delighted. Not by him specifically. By anything. If your whole range has flattened, that's not progress, that's armour.

Here's what I want you to know, because I think this is the most important thing in this email.

The goal was never to need less. It was to be able to hold the needing without it making your decisions.

A woman who wants a man deeply and still walks away from a bad arrangement is enormously powerful. A woman who feels nothing and walks away has just avoided a situation. Those look the same from outside. They are not the same life.

What actually works.

Let yourself want things and do the regulating in your body instead of in your heart. Four in, eight out. Not a wall.

Check your range. Can you still be moved by a song, a friend's good news, a bit of weather? If not, the numbness has generalised.

And don't use my work as armour. Everything I teach is for having the thing, not for never being hurt again. Those are different projects and the second one has a very high price.

You are not trying to stop wanting him. You're trying to be a woman whose wanting doesn't decide anything.

Your one move today.

Let yourself feel one thing fully today without managing it. Twenty minutes.

Then notice you survived it.

Have you ever gone numb and called it healing? Reply, I did it for most of a year.
