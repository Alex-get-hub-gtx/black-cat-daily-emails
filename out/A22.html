Why you feel calm around men you do not want

The spark might be a warning.

Most women use the spark as their main instrument.

If there's a spark, he's right. If there's no spark, he's nice but boring. Entire years get decided on it.

Here's what the spark actually is a lot of the time.

Anxiety and attraction share a chemistry, and your body cannot reliably tell you which one you're in.

Racing heart. Butterflies. Can't eat. Can't stop thinking about him. Checking your phone. That heightened, electric, alive feeling.

Now read that list again as a description of fear. Every single symptom is identical.

Your body produces the arousal and then your mind goes looking for a cause. If there's an attractive man nearby, he becomes the cause. The feeling gets labelled chemistry. And it is genuinely intense, and it is genuinely not information about whether he's good for you.

Here's the part that reorganises everything.

The men who make you feel the most are often the men whose behaviour is the least predictable. Inconsistency produces activation. Activation feels like passion. So the man who keeps you slightly off balance generates more "chemistry" than the man who is simply, reliably there.

Which means your spark meter is partly a volatility meter. And you've been using it to pick husbands.

Meanwhile the man who calls when he says he will, who is warm and steady and available, produces no activation at all. Your body is calm around him. And you read calm as nothing.

Calm is not nothing. Calm is what safe feels like when you are not used to it.

Here's the scenario.

Two men. One texts erratically, cancels, comes back harder, makes her feel insane in both directions. She's obsessed.

The other one is consistent, kind, clearly interested. She says there's no spark. She's not wrong about the feeling. She's wrong about what the feeling measures.

What actually works.

Separate the two questions. Ask "what is my body doing" and then separately ask "what has this man actually done". Two different instruments. Use both.

Give calm four dates. Not one. Attraction to a steady man builds through familiarity rather than arriving at the door, and most women write him off before the mechanism has a chance to run.

And when you feel the big spark, get curious instead of excited. Ask yourself: is this interest, or is this uncertainty? One of those is a reason to continue. The other is a reason to be careful.

You have been taught to trust the feeling that goes fastest. The feeling that goes fastest is usually fear wearing something nice.

Your one move today.

Think of the man who produced the most chemistry you've ever felt. Now write down how predictable his behaviour was.
