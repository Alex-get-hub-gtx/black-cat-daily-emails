Your inner world is the thing he cannot buy

Stop narrating all of it.

Most women think closeness means total disclosure.

So she tells him everything. What she's thinking, what she's worried about, what she's feeling about him, what her friend said about him, what she thinks it means. She's being open, and openness is supposed to be good.

Here's what's actually happening.

You're closing the only question he has about you.

Attraction requires something unresolved. Not a game, not a secret. Just a part of you that's still being worked out, that he can sense is there, that he does not have full access to.

The moment a man has complete information about your inner life, there's nothing left to approach. He knows how much you like him, he knows what you're worried about, he knows how you'll react. You have become fully mapped. And a fully mapped person is a settled question, and settled questions don't occupy anyone.

This gets confused with being closed off, so let me be exact about the difference.

Closed off is: I won't tell you anything, I don't trust you, there's a wall. That's cold and it kills things too.

What I'm describing is: I have a rich inner life that is mine, and you get a lot of it, and you don't get a running commentary on all of it. That's warm and it's bottomless.

The difference is whether there's something in there, not whether the door is open.

Here's where it leaks most.

Narrating your feelings about him, to him, in real time. "I was thinking about you all day." "I was a bit worried when you didn't reply." "I really like you, is that too much?" Each one is a small transfer of information about exactly where you are, and he adjusts accordingly. A man who knows precisely how much you like him has no question to answer.

Processing out loud. The thinking that should happen in your own head, or with a friend, happening in his messages instead. He becomes your processor, which is intimate and also puts him permanently on the inside of your decisions about him.

And over-reporting your day. Every detail, unasked. It's not that he doesn't care. It's that nothing is left for him to wonder about or ask for.

What actually works.

Keep ten percent back. Not a secret, just unreported. A thought you didn't share, a day he doesn't get the full version of. You'll feel it immediately, this strange sense of having something of your own.

Process with your friends, land with him. Do the churn elsewhere. Bring him the conclusion, not the working out.

Don't answer "what are you thinking" fully. "I'll tell you later" is a perfectly good answer and it will stay with him all evening.

And have thoughts about things other than the relationship. This is the real one. A woman with opinions about the world is inexhaustible. A woman whose entire inner life is the relationship has one subject, and he's already read it.

Mystery is not information you withhold. It's an inner life that keeps moving whether he's looking or not.

Your one move today.

Have one thought today that you don't tell him. Just notice how it feels to keep it.

That feeling is the thing you've been missing.

Do you tell him everything? Reply honestly, I used to.
