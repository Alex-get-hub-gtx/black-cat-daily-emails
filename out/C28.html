The long game of being unbothered

Sustainable or suppressed?

You've learned not to react. And there are two completely different ways to not react, and from the outside they're identical.

Here's the difference, and it determines whether this lasts five years or eighteen months.

Sustainable non-reactivity: the feeling arrives, your body processes it, and you choose your response. It costs almost nothing because the feeling has somewhere to go.

Suppression: the feeling arrives and you hold it down. It costs a great deal, continuously, and the cost accumulates somewhere else.

Both produce a woman who doesn't react. Only one of them is survivable.

Here's how to tell which you're running.

Where does it come out? Suppressed feeling doesn't disappear, it relocates. Sleep, jaw, stomach, shoulders, a short temper with someone safe, a glass of wine that's become two. If you're perfectly composed with him and snapping at your sister, that's suppression.

How tired are you? Suppression is metabolically expensive. A woman holding things down all day is exhausted and usually can't explain why.

Can you still be surprised by delight? Suppression generalises. It's not selective, and the capacity to hold down the hard feelings will eventually flatten the good ones too.

And the clearest one: is there anywhere it goes? Sustainable non-reactivity requires an outlet. If there's genuinely nowhere the feeling gets expressed, it's being stored.

Here's what makes it sustainable, and it's three things.

Regulation rather than suppression in the moment. The long exhale, the pause, the body. That's processing, not holding.

An outlet elsewhere. A friend, a page, a therapist, a run. The composure with him is only affordable if the feeling has somewhere else to be. This is the piece almost everyone is missing, and it's why so many women can do this brilliantly for a year and then come apart.

And the thing underneath: if nothing ever actually changes, no amount of regulation will hold. Composure is for choosing your response, not for tolerating something indefinitely. A woman using her regulation skills to make an unacceptable situation survivable is using an excellent tool for the wrong job, and it will cost her her health.

That last one is the most important sentence in this email.

What actually works.

Check where it's going. Sleep, body, temper, someone safe. That's your diagnostic.

Build the outlet deliberately. One place the feelings get fully expressed, weekly, without management.

And check the composure isn't doing a job it shouldn't. If you've been unbothered about the same thing for a year and the thing hasn't changed, the problem isn't your reactivity.

Being unbothered is only sustainable if the feeling goes somewhere. If it's going into your jaw and your sleep, you have not become regulated, you've become quiet.

Your one move today.

Where is it going? Be honest. Sleep, stomach, jaw, temper, drinking.

That's your answer about which one you're running.
