Avoidant, or just not interested

The two week test.

Most women have read about avoidant attachment and it has cost them a year.

Because it gave her an explanation for behaviour that didn't need one. He pulls away when things get close. He's scared of intimacy. He's got a wound, and if she's patient and secure enough he'll come toward her eventually.

Sometimes that's true. Far more often, the attachment theory was doing the job of making indifference bearable.

Here's the distinction and it's important.

An avoidant man who wants you is in conflict. A man who doesn't want you is not in conflict at all.

That's the whole test. Conflict looks like something. Indifference looks like nothing.

A genuinely avoidant man who is attached to you will come close and then pull back and then come close again. It's a cycle and it's visible. He'll be unusually intense and then unavailable. He'll say something revealing and then go quiet for three days. He'll reach for you and then flinch. He is clearly struggling with something and the struggle is the evidence.

A man who isn't interested doesn't do any of that. He's just consistently, calmly, low effort. There's no push and pull because there's no push. He's warm when you're in front of him and he does not think about you otherwise. That's not a fear of intimacy. That's an absence of desire.

Here's the two week test, and it's simple.

Stop initiating for two weeks. Completely. No texts, no plans, nothing.

The avoidant man who's attached will come back, usually between day four and day ten, often intensely. The distance is what he needed and your absence creates it. He'll reappear and it'll be warm and a bit confusing.

The uninterested man will not come back at all. And that's your answer, and it took two weeks rather than a year.

Now here's the part I have to say plainly.

Even if he is avoidant and attached, that is not automatically good news.

An avoidant man is not a project. You cannot love someone out of it. The cycle you're observing will continue for as long as you're in it, and your role in it will be to absorb the pulling away and be grateful for the coming back.

The only version that works is a man doing his own work on it. Not you being patient enough.

What actually works.

Run the two weeks. It's the cleanest piece of information available to you and it costs you nothing you weren't already not getting.

Look for conflict, not for distance. Distance alone means nothing.

And if he's avoidant and attached, ask yourself the real question: is he working on it, or am I?

A man who is frightened of how much he wants you behaves very differently from a man who doesn't want you. One of them is visibly struggling. The other one is just calm.

Your one move today.

If you're in this, start the two weeks today. Mark the date.
