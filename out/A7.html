Attention is not attraction

What does it actually cost him?

Most women read attention as evidence.

He texts every day. He watches every story. He replies within minutes. So he must be interested, and when he still doesn't commit it feels like a contradiction.

It's not a contradiction. You're measuring the wrong thing.

Here's the frame that fixes this permanently.

In biology there's a concept called costly signalling. The idea is simple: a signal only carries information if it's expensive to fake. A peacock's tail is honest because a sick bird cannot grow one. A cheap signal carries almost no information at all, because anyone can send it.

Now rank what a man does for you by what it costs him.

A text costs him nothing. He sent it from the sofa. Watching your story costs him nothing, it was already on his screen. A compliment costs nothing. "We should do something soon" costs absolutely nothing, and it's the single most common thing a man says to a woman he has no plans for.

Now the expensive ones. Driving to you. Introducing you to his friends. Changing a plan he'd already made. Telling you something true about himself that makes him look bad. Making a decision he can't quietly reverse.

Those cost him. Those are the only ones that mean anything.

Here's the scenario, and I promise you know this woman.

He texts her all day. Every day. Memes, questions, how was your morning. She feels wanted constantly. Four months in she realises she has never met a single one of his friends and every plan has been made and moved by him twice.

She has been receiving an enormous volume of cheap signals and reading the volume as depth.

Volume is not depth. A hundred free things still cost nothing.

What actually works.

Rate behaviour by cost, not by frequency. Ask yourself one question about anything he does: what did that cost him? If the answer is nothing, file it as nothing. Pleasant, but nothing.

Stop paying for cheap signals with expensive ones. If he sends a text, send a text. Don't answer a meme with your emotional availability. Match the currency.

And watch what happens to the cheap signals when you stop responding to them as if they were gifts. Either they upgrade or they stop. Both are useful.

Anyone can text you. Only a man who wants you will inconvenience himself for you.

Your one move today.

Take the last week. Write down everything he actually did, and next to each one write what it cost him. Time, money, social risk, effort, nothing.

Look at the column. That's your real answer, and you already had it.
