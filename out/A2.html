Most women get attraction backwards

You cannot give your way into being wanted.

Most women treat attraction like a bank account.

Give more, get more. Be more generous, more understanding, more available, and eventually the interest shows up.

It doesn't work like that. It has never worked like that.

Attraction is not a debt a man repays. It's a state you create and he responds to.

Here's the mechanism.

Feelings are not caused by facts. They're caused by physical state plus the story the brain attaches to it. Your heart rate goes up, your body goes looking for a reason, and whoever is standing in front of you becomes the reason. Researchers have known this for decades and dating has never caught up with it.

Which means attraction is not an argument you win by presenting better evidence about how good you'd be for him.

It's contrast. It's not knowing. It's a slightly elevated nervous system with his face attached to it.

And generosity flattens all of it.

When you're endlessly available, endlessly agreeable, endlessly reachable, there is no contrast. There is no gap. There's nothing for his body to do except settle. And a settled body does not fall in love. It gets comfortable.

Here's the scenario I see constantly.

She likes him. So she doubles her effort. Faster replies, more interest in his hobbies, cancelling her own plans to fit his schedule. She's being lovely. Genuinely lovely.

And he gets less interested every single week, and she cannot work out why, because by every measure she can think of she's being a better and better option.

She's not losing because she's giving too much. She's losing because giving is not the input. The input is state.

What actually works.

Vary. Be warm on Tuesday and busy on Thursday, not as a tactic, because you actually have a Thursday. The variation is the whole point.

Bring the elevated state yourself. Go to him from the gym, from a night out, from something that already has you lit up. Men do not fall for your personality, they fall for the state they find you in.

And stop closing gaps. When there's a pause in the conversation, leave it. When you don't know where you stand, sit in it for one more day. Every gap you rush to fill is a gap his imagination was about to fill for you.

Nobody has ever been talked into wanting someone. They've only ever been given room to.

Your one move today.

Find one gap and leave it. A text you'd normally answer in eight seconds, answer in two hours. Not to punish him. To practise the feeling of not closing it.

That tightness you feel while you wait? That's the muscle. It gets stronger.

Tell me, what's harder for you, leaving a text unanswered or leaving a question unasked? Reply, I want to know.

See you tomorrow!
