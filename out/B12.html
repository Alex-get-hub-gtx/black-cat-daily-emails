Reconditioning a man who took you for granted

One thing has to change first.

You've been in it for two years and he's comfortable. He's not cruel, he's just stopped trying, and you want to know whether it can be turned around.

It can. Often. But there's one condition and nearly everyone gets it wrong.

Your life has to change before his behaviour will.

Here's why, and it's not mystical.

A man's effort is a response to conditions. Right now the conditions are: she's always there, she organises everything, her week revolves around mine, and nothing I do or don't do alters any of that.

Under those conditions, effort is irrational. Not unkind. Irrational. There's no input that produces a different output, so the sensible move is to spend nothing.

Which means you cannot recondition him by asking, because asking doesn't change any condition. And you cannot do it by being better, because being better just improves what he already gets for free.

The only thing that changes his behaviour is a change in what is available.

And here's the part women resist, because it sounds unfair: that change has to be real. You can't manufacture it for six weeks. He's known you for two years and he can tell the difference between a woman who's busy and a woman who's performing busy.

So the sequence is this.

First, fill your week. Genuinely. Two or three fixed commitments that are not about him and that you will not move. This takes about a month to establish and it is the whole foundation.

Second, stop supplying the invisible labour. The organising, the remembering, the emotional management, the planning. Not announced, just stopped. Let the gaps be gaps.

Third, hold the level while it gets worse. And it will get worse before it changes, usually for about two weeks, because systems resist. He may get irritable. He may accuse you of being distant. This is the moment nearly everyone folds, and folding here costs you the entire run.

Fourth, when he steps up, let it land. Warmth, immediately, no testing. This is where it gets locked in.

Here's the honest part.

It doesn't always work. Sometimes a man is comfortable because he's decided you're the arrangement and not the woman, and no change in conditions will move him.

But you'll know inside about six weeks, which is much better than another two years of asking.

What actually works.

Change your life first, his behaviour second. In that order, never the reverse.

Expect the dip at week two and plan for it.

And reward the step up immediately and visibly.

He did not stop trying because he stopped caring. He stopped because nothing he did changed what he got, and that is a condition you can actually alter.

Your one move today.

Book the two fixed commitments. This week.

Everything else in this email depends on that one thing being real.
