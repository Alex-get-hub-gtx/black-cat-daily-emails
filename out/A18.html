The real reason he came back

It's not what it feels like.

Most women read a man coming back as proof.

He realised what he lost. He couldn't stop thinking about her. All those weeks of pain meant something after all.

Here's the harder read, and you need it, because what you do in the next week decides everything.

A man coming back is information about his options. It's not usually information about your worth.

Men do not generally sit with an absence and arrive at insight. They sit with an absence until something else fails. The other thing didn't work out, the single life got boring, the person he was actually pursuing said no. And in that moment, the thing he knows is available becomes attractive again.

There's a name for part of this. Reactance. When something is taken away, our valuation of it jumps, purely because it's been removed. It's not a revelation about the thing. It's a reaction to losing access.

Which means the warmth you're feeling right now is real, and it's also the cheapest kind of signal there is. It cost him one text.

Now here's what matters. I am not telling you he can't be genuine. Some men come back and build something real. I'm telling you that you cannot tell the difference from the returning, and most women decide at the returning.

So don't decide at the returning. Decide at the four week mark.

Here's what the two versions look like.

The genuine one comes back and the terms change. He's more available, not less. He makes something concrete happen quickly, a plan, an introduction, a decision. He names what went wrong without being asked. He's working.

The option one comes back and the terms are identical. Same access, same vagueness, same lovely energy, same nothing. And within a month you're exactly where you were, except now you've taught him he can leave and return at no cost.

That last part is the real risk. A man who leaves and comes back to the same welcome has learned the price of leaving is zero.

What actually works.

Be warm and slow. Warmth costs you nothing and keeps your dignity. Speed is what costs you. Don't go from nothing to everything in a weekend because it feels so good to be wanted again.

Make the terms different. Whatever he had before, he does not automatically have now. Not as punishment. Because the thing that existed before ended, and this is a new thing with new pricing.

And watch for effort, not feeling. He will have a lot of feeling. Feeling is free.

He didn't come back because he finally understood your value. He came back because something else closed. What he does in the next four weeks is the only real answer.

Your one move today.

If he's back, or if he comes back, decide right now what one thing has to be different this time. One. Concrete. Then hold it.

Mine would be: the first plan is his to make, and I don't help him make it.

Has one ever come back? What did you do? Reply, I read all of these.
