The conversations to have before a year

Four, in this order.

If this is going somewhere, there are four conversations that have to happen before the first year is out. In this order, each one spaced by weeks, each one short.

One. Children.

Not "how do you feel about kids". Do you want them, and roughly when. Closed question, asked calmly, once. This goes first because it's the only one that is genuinely non-negotiable, and every month you spend not knowing is a month you may be spending on the wrong life.

Two. Where you live.

Country, city, proximity to family. People treat this as practical and flexible and it is neither. A man who intends to move back to his hometown eventually has told you something enormous and he may never mention it unless asked.

Three. Money.

Not how much he has. How he thinks about it. Does he spend or save, is he in debt, what does he think a couple should share. You are not auditing him, you're finding out whether your two approaches can live together, and incompatible money philosophies end more relationships than infidelity does.

Four. What a good life looks like.

This is the biggest and it goes last because it needs the trust the others build. What does a good Saturday look like in ten years. How much work, how much rest, how social, how ambitious, how quiet. Two people can agree on children, city and money and still want entirely different lives.

Here's how to have them.

The four sentence structure, every time. Context line, closed question, silence, acknowledgement. Ninety seconds each.

Spaced out. Not one evening. One a month across four months, in ordinary moments, not in a Serious Talk.

And no reacting in the room. Take the answer away and decide about it alone. This is the rule that makes honest answers possible, because a man who gets argued with the first time will manage the second time.

Here's what not to do.

Don't make them a negotiation. You're gathering facts, not persuading him toward a position. A man talked into wanting children does not want children.

Don't ask them all at once, and don't ask them in the first three months. Too early and you'll get the answer he thinks you want, because he doesn't know you well enough yet to risk the real one.

And believe the first answer. Always.

You can be a year in, in love, and discover that the two of you want entirely different lives. That is avoidable, and it is avoided in four short conversations nobody wants to have.

Your one move today.

Which of the four have you never asked? Pick one.

Have it this month. Four sentences.
