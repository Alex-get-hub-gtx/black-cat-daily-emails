How to handle his ex

Three responses that end it.

She comes up. In a story, in a photo, in a mutual friend's wedding, in his phone.

And whatever you do next either ends her presence in your relationship or installs her in it permanently.

Here's the mechanism, and it's not about her at all.

The amount of space an ex occupies is determined almost entirely by how much attention you give her.

A name mentioned once and received calmly is a name. The same name met with questions, a change in your tone, a quiet mood for the rest of the evening, becomes a subject. And subjects grow.

And there's a second effect that's worse. Visible insecurity about an ex invites comparison. You have just told him there's a competition, and he hadn't been running one.

Here's what to do, in three situations.

When he mentions her. Receive it exactly like any other name. No tightening, no follow-up questions, no change in energy. "Right" and carry on. If it's a story, let it be a story. The goal is that mentioning her is unremarkable, because the moment it's remarkable, he'll either avoid it, which creates secrecy, or lean on it, which creates leverage.

When you find yourself wanting to ask. Don't ask him, ask yourself what you want to know. It's almost never information. It's reassurance that you're better, or that it's over, and neither of those can be supplied by an answer about her. If you genuinely need a fact, use the four sentences. Once.

When she's actually still around. This is different and it's a behaviour question, not a feelings question. Not "how do you feel about her" but "are you in contact, how often, and does she know about me." Closed questions, asked once, calmly. Then you judge the answer on behaviour over the following weeks.

And the thing that never works: competing. Being more fun, more agreeable, more sexual, more available, in order to beat someone who isn't there. You cannot win a competition against a memory, because memories improve and you're real.

Here's the one that's hardest.

Stop looking at her online. Every single look installs her a bit further. You're not gathering information, you're running a comparison you've already decided you'll lose.

What actually works.

Be unbothered in the room, every time her name comes up.

Ask about behaviour, never about feelings, and only if there's a real fact you need.

And close the tab. Permanently.

She only lives in your relationship if you keep bringing her in. He mentioned a name. You can make it a name or you can make it a subject.

Your one move today.

If you've been looking, stop today. Block or mute if you need to.

That single action does more than any conversation about her ever will.

Is there one still in your relationship? Reply and tell me how she got there.
