How to slow down physically

Access is not attachment.

Most women are afraid that if they wait, he'll lose interest.

So she doesn't wait, and then she spends the next three weeks trying to work out why the energy changed, and she decides it was something she said.

It wasn't something she said.

Here's the mechanism, and I want to be very clear that this is not about morality. I don't care what you do. I care about what works.

Attachment is built by time and investment. Access does not build it, and giving access early removes the conditions under which it gets built.

Here's why. Everything I've told you about how a man bonds runs on repeated effort over time. Vasopressin pathways, effort justification, loss aversion, the whole architecture requires a period in which he is working toward something.

That period is the only window you get. It cannot be recreated afterwards.

If the thing he's working toward arrives on night one, the window closes before anything was built in it. He now has the outcome without the process. And the feeling he has about you is based on one night, which is novelty, which expires.

This is why the pattern is so consistent and so brutal. Intense for two or three weeks, then the fade, and she cannot understand it because it was so good.

It was good. It was also the whole thing, spent at the start.

And there's a second mechanism. Anticipation produces more of the chemical that creates wanting than attainment does. The wanting is in the waiting. A man who is anticipating you is, chemically, more invested than a man who isn't, and the anticipation is doing work that nothing else can do.

Here's what I'd want you to hold.

A man who loses interest because you didn't sleep with him fast enough was never going to bond with you anyway. He was running a different programme. Losing him costs you nothing except the three weeks you'd have spent finding out the hard way.

That's not a consolation. It's genuinely the best use of the information.

What actually works.

Let the physical pace follow the investment, not the chemistry. Chemistry is available on night one. Investment takes weeks. Follow the slower one.

Be warm while you're slow. This is the part people get wrong. Slow and cold reads as rejection. Slow and warm, genuinely affectionate, clearly attracted, just not yet, is the thing that makes a man lose his mind.

And never explain or negotiate the timeline. No rules speech, no "I don't do this until". Just don't. "Not tonight" with a smile is complete.

He does not bond to the night he got you. He bonds to the weeks in which he was trying to.

Your one move today.

Nothing to do today. Just hold this one: the three weeks of wanting are not an obstacle before the relationship. They are where the relationship is built.

Has rushing ever worked out for you? Honest answers only. Reply, this one is always interesting.

See you tomorrow!
